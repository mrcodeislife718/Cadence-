# Cadence — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** Backend / developer infrastructure

Cadence is complete only when its backend/runtime responsibilities are executable, reliable under load and dependency failure, observable, secure, and measurably useful to the surrounding platform.

Required proof: end-to-end request/workflow tests; state/idempotency/concurrency coverage; dependency/network/storage failure injection; benchmarks for throughput, latency, saturation, memory, and recovery; auth/secrets/security controls; repeatable deployment, health checks, telemetry, rollback, and operational runbooks.

**Next proof target:** define one canonical platform workload, load it to saturation, inject dependency failures, and preserve correctness, tail-latency, recovery, and resource-use evidence.
